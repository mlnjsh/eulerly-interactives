What to try

  • Drop b into every quadrant. Which side of the green and red dividing line gives a backward shadow?
  • Drag b until the shadow shrinks to a dot. What is a · b there?
  • Press a × ½ and a × 2. The scalar projection and the shadow do not move: a only supplies a direction.
  • Press make a a unit vector. Now ‖a‖ = 1, so the scalar projection is just a · b.
  • Press flip a. The shadow arrow stays put, but the scalar projection changes sign. Why?
