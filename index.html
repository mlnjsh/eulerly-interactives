Linear algebra you can touch

Free interactive explorations from the course Applied Linear Algebra: The Math Behind AI and Beyond. Drag, turn the dials, and watch the maths move. Every page shows the matching line of NumPy.

Module 2 · Vectors: The Toolkit

Lesson 2.2 · Length and norms

Norm explorer

Drag a vector and compare three ways to measure it: the drone (L2), the rider (L1) and the crane (L∞), with their circle, diamond and square.
Lesson 2.3 · Linear combinations

Linear combination dials

Turn the weights m and n, leave a dot at every setting, fill the plane, and hit targets. Drag a and b to discover when the span collapses to a line.

Module 3 · Dot Products, Angles and Similarity

Lesson 3.3 · Cosine similarity

Cosine similarity dial

Drag two arrows and watch a dial swing from −1 (opposite) through 0 (unrelated) to +1 (alike). Stretch an arrow and see that the needle does not move.
Lesson 3.4 · Projections

Projection explorer

Cast the shadow of b on the line of a: scalar projection, vector projection, and a remainder that always meets a at a right angle. Drop b behind a for a backward shadow.
