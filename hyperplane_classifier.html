Hyperplane classifier

One equation, w · x + b = 0, draws a line and splits the plane in two. The vector w stands at a right angle to the line and points into the + side. Plug any point in: the sign of its score w · x + b says which side it is on, and dividing by ‖w‖ turns the score into a real distance.

Selected points

Click a point to select or deselect it. Each selected point shows its score and its signed distance (negative on the − side). With more than four selected, only P and N keep their labels on the plane; the table lists them all.

What to try

  • Read the two default points: P scores +10 and sits 2 units on the blue side, N scores −5 and sits 1 unit on the coral side.
  • Press double w and b. The line does not move, the scores double, and the distances stay put: dividing by ‖w‖ undoes the stretch.
  • Drag the tip of w. The line turns so that it always stays at a right angle to the arrow.
  • Set b = 0. The line now passes through the origin, and the origin's score is 0.
  • Add a random cloud and count the points on each side. Which points are far from the line, and which are close?
