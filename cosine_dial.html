Cosine similarity dial

Drag the tips of a and b. The dial reads cos θ = a · b / (‖a‖ ‖b‖): +1 same direction, 0 unrelated (a right angle), −1 opposite. It compares directions only, so stretching an arrow never moves the needle.

What to try

  • Keep a fixed and swing b once around it. Where does the needle cross 0? Where does it hit −1?
  • Press b × 2 a few times. a · b and ‖b‖ grow, cos θ does not.
  • Find two whole-number arrows with cos θ exactly 0 that are not on the axes.
  • Load "Short vs long article". The tips are far apart, yet the score is exactly 1.
  • Tick "lock directions" and drag a tip: only the length changes, the dial never moves.
